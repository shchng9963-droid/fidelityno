Final independent-weight known-rate readout sensitivity, 15 October 2026

Requires the data/code assets at:
https://github.com/shchng9963-droid/fidelityno/releases
Extract mlst-revision-20261002, mlst-independent-weights-20261002 and
mlst-closeout-20261003, in that order, then overlay this small supplement.

This is a secondary sensitivity run on the final stage-five query sets.
It does not generate a new independent test, retrain source checkpoints,
change validation guards, select new budgets or add significance tests.
The 20 calibration groups and five label repeats are retained. At nonzero
known flip rates, calibration labels and query measurements are redrawn.
The supplied rate is exact; rate-estimation costs are outside this test.
Every prior still pays 4096 calibration shots per fitted deployment.

The variance of attenuated +/-1 signs is estimated only from calibration
outcomes. Divide this pooled variance by attenuation squared and the actual
query setting counts. Final query outcomes do not enter the weights.
Zero-flip streams reproduce all corresponding final primary MAEs within 1e-12.
The design, input/code hashes and clean regression check are recorded.

The zero-flip streams match the primary evaluation. Positive-rate stream seed
is 202610151 and is separated by family, rate, group, repeat, budget and purpose.

Saved outputs per family: runs.csv (all individual-fit MAEs), summary.csv,
calibration_moments.json and manifest.json. Raw outcomes are exactly regenerable
from the supplied seeds and existing physical-query expectations. Results are
descriptive mean risks and run-to-run variation, not new target confirmations.

At 5% flips, dephasing neural MAEs at 20/36 shots are 0.06415/0.05465;
ridge gives 0.06796/0.05686. DFE at 32/48 shots gives 0.07259/0.05922.
Exchange zero-query ridge gives 0.05176. The earlier stage-three readout
results remain historical and use different estimators and query sets.
