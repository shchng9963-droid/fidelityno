GUARD AND CLIPPING
The mean/median use raw finite-shot labels. Labels and query DFE estimates are
not clipped in the measurement routine. Ridge and affine prior predictions are
clipped before fusion and out-of-fold residual estimation. The residual moment
subtracts the outcome-estimated label variance before truncation at zero. The
fusion is finally clipped before scoring. Pilot-labelled budgets below 15 use
balanced counts, drawing no pilot. Other pilots cost nine shots per query.
The guard sums sample-variance components of query means /4096, calibration-group
means /20, and (neural only) checkpoint means /5, then adds 1.645 sqrt(sum) to MAE.
It is a selection heuristic, distinct from independent-test bootstrap inference.
